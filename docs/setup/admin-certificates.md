# Certificates for members

The portal does not create certificates. An Admin or Secretary prepares one
outside the portal — a PDF or an image — and uploads it to the profile of the
member who volunteered, or to their child's section of it.

## Upload one

1. Open **Members**, then the member (for a child's certificate, the parent).
2. In **Certificates**, choose **Upload certificate**.
3. Give it a title, pick the child if it is for one, and choose the file.

The member — or the parent, for a child's — then finds it on **My profile**
and can download and print it. See the User Guide:
[Certificates](/guide/certificates.md).

## Who can see a certificate

Only the member it is filed under, the other parent in the same household for
a child's, and the officers who can upload. Nobody else — another member
asking for it is told it does not exist.

Use **Delete** to withdraw one uploaded by mistake.
