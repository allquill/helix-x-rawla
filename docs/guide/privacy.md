# Privacy

Nothing in the portal is public. The directory, the list of people attending
an event and the Top Volunteers list can only be seen by signed-in members.

**My profile → Privacy** controls what those members see of you.

## Being listed in the directory

Turn **List me in the member directory** off to hide your whole record from
other members. When it is off:

- other members cannot find you in the directory;
- on an event's list of participants, you — and your spouse and children — are
  shown as "Private member". You are still counted;
- on Top Volunteers, your household's volunteers are shown as "Private
  volunteer", with their hours.

Officers can still see your record.

## Hiding single fields

Stay in the directory but hide particular details: phone number, WhatsApp
number, date of birth, wedding anniversary, job title, industry, education,
family history, LinkedIn and Facebook.

## Event emails

Turn **Email me event invitations and reminders** off to stop:

- invitations when a new event is announced;
- reminders before events you are registered for.

This applies to you and to your spouse's address. You will still get
confirmations for registrations and payments you make.

## What nobody else ever sees

Your payments, what you owe or paid for an event, and your food, T-shirt and
hotel details are visible only to you and to the officers running the event.
