# Certificates

Certificates — for volunteering, for example — are prepared by the organisers
and added to your profile.

## Finding and downloading them

Open **My profile** and scroll to **Certificates**. You will see your own, and
those of your children with the child's name. Choose **Download** to save one,
then print it or attach it to a school or college application.

## Who can see them

Only you, the other parent in your household for a child's certificate, and
the officers who add them.

## Expecting one that is not there

Certificates are added by hand after an event has been closed, so it can take
a little while. If it has been some time, [contact us](/guide/contact.md).
