# Questions and answers

## Joining

**I applied but have heard nothing.**
Check that you confirmed your email — your application is only reviewed after
that. Then see **My membership** for where it stands.
[Joining](/guide/joining.md)

**My confirmation link has expired.**
Try to sign in; you will be offered a new one.
[Signing in](/guide/signing-in.md)

**I have paid but I am not active yet.**
All three steps must be complete: email, approval and dues. **My membership**
shows which is outstanding.

## Profile

**How do I change my name, gotra or chapter?**
Those are kept by the Membership Secretary. [Contact us](/guide/contact.md).

**How do I add my spouse or a child?**
**My profile → Household**. [Your profile and household](/guide/profile.md)

**Who can see my phone number?**
Signed-in members, unless you hide it. [Privacy](/guide/privacy.md)

## Events

**Can I register a friend or a relative who is not a member?**
No. Only people in your own household can be registered.

**Why did my child get an adult ticket?**
Tickets go by age on the day of the event, and each event sets its own age
groups. They are listed on the event page under **Attendance fee**.

**I registered at the early-bird price but have not paid yet. Will it go up?**
No. The price is fixed when you register.

**Can I cancel?**
Before paying, yes. After paying, not in the portal — contact us, and note
that refunds are not handled in the portal.
[Registering for an event](/guide/registering.md#cancelling)

**I paid by Zelle and my registration still says "Awaiting payment".**
An administrator records Zelle payments by hand. It will change once they have.

**I do not want event emails.**
**My profile → Privacy → Event emails**. [Privacy](/guide/privacy.md#event-emails)

## Volunteering

**How do I choose what I will do?**
You do not, in the portal. Tick "volunteer" when registering; the organisers
assign activities and contact you.

**My hours are not showing.**
Hours are entered after the event and appear once the event is closed.
[Volunteering](/guide/volunteering.md)
