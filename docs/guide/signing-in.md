# Signing in

Choose **Sign in** and enter the email address you applied with and your
password.

## Forgotten your password

On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password**, enter your email address, and
open the link we send. It is valid for a short time only.

## Changing your password

When signed in, open your account menu (top right) → **Security**.

## If you cannot sign in

The sign-in page tells you why and what to do:

| It says | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your email is not confirmed | Use the link offered to send the confirmation email again |
| Your application was not approved | Contact the Membership Secretary |
| Your account has been archived | Contact the Membership Secretary |
| The email or password is wrong | Try again, or reset your password |
| Too many attempts | Wait a few minutes and try again |

If you are waiting for approval or have not yet paid your dues, you **can**
sign in — you will see your membership status page, with what is still to do.

## Signing out

Account menu → **Sign out**. If an officer tells you that you have been given
a new role or access to something, sign out and back in to pick it up.
