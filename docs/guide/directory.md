# The member directory

**Members** lists the active members of the community. Search by name, and
open a member to see their profile.

You see what each member has chosen to share: their name and chapter, and
whichever of their contact, work and family details they have not hidden.
Members who have left the directory do not appear.

To decide what others see of you, go to [Privacy](/guide/privacy.md).

The directory is available once your own membership is active.
