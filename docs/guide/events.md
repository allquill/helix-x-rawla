# Events

**Events** shows what is coming up, as a gallery of flyers. Switch to **Past**
for events that have taken place.

Each card shows the kind of event, the chapter running it, the date, the
place, the lowest ticket price and whether registration is open.

When a new event is announced you also get an invitation by email, unless you
have [turned event emails off](/guide/privacy.md#event-emails).

## The event page

Open an event to see:

- **When and where** — the date and time (in the event's own time zone), how
  long it lasts and the location
- **Maximum participants** — or "No maximum" — and how many places are left
- **Register by** — the last day to register
- **Attendance fee** — a price for each age group; an early-bird price is
  marked, with the price it changes to
- the **flyer** and description
- what to wear, if the organisers have said
- after the event, a link to the **photos**

## Who is taking part

At the bottom of the event page is the list of everyone registered, grouped by
household, with volunteers marked. Members who are not in the directory are
counted but shown as "Private member".

The list never shows what anyone paid or chose.

## Registering

If registration is open you will see **Register**. See
[Registering for an event](/guide/registering.md).

If your household is already registered, the page links to your registration
instead.
