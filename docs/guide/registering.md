# Registering for an event

You register your whole household in one go, with one payment. Open the event
and choose **Register**.

Registration is open until the end of the **Register by** date, as long as
places remain.

## Step 1 — Who is coming

Tick everyone in your household who will attend: yourself, your spouse, your
children. Beside each person is the ticket they get and its price.

- The ticket depends on the person's **age on the day of the event**. You do
  not choose it.
- An **Early bird** price applies if you register before its deadline.
- Someone is missing? Add them to [your household](/guide/profile.md#your-household)
  first, then come back.
- Only people in your household can be registered.

## Step 2 — Details

For each person:

- **Food preference**, and any allergies
- **T-shirt size** and **hotel details**, if the organisers ask for them
- **Will volunteer at this event** — tick this for anyone who will help,
  including children. Activities are assigned later and the organisers will be
  in touch; see [Volunteering](/guide/volunteering.md).

## Step 3 — Waiver

Some events ask you to accept a liability waiver. Read it, tick that you
accept it, and type your full name to sign. You sign for yourself and, as
parent or guardian, for your children.

## Step 4 — Time slots

Some events have appointments — a blood-donation time, for example. Book one
for anyone who wants it. Each has a limited number of places.

## Step 5 — Review and pay

Check the summary and the total, then choose **Register and pay**. You are
taken to a secure payment page and brought back to your registration.

The price is fixed when you register: if you registered at the early-bird
price, that is what you pay even if you pay later.

A free event needs no payment — your places are confirmed straight away.

## Paying another way

If the organisers accept **Zelle**, the instructions are on the event page and
on your registration. Pay as described; an administrator records your payment
when it arrives, and your registration then shows as confirmed. Until then
your places are held.

## Your registration

**Events → the event → View my registration** shows who is registered, what
you chose, what you have paid and any balance.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting payment | Your places are held; there is a balance to pay |
| Confirmed | Paid in full, or nothing to pay |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it |
| Removed | An administrator removed it |

Use **Pay now** to pay a balance.

A few days before the event you get a reminder by email with the time, the
place, what to wear and your time slots.

## Cancelling

- **Before you have paid**, use **Cancel registration**. Your places are
  released for others, and you can register again later if places remain.
- **After you have paid, you cannot cancel in the portal.** If your plans
  change, [contact us](/guide/contact.md); an administrator can remove your
  registration. Refunds are not handled in the portal.

## If something stops you

| You are told | Why |
|---|---|
| Registration has closed | The last day to register has passed |
| There are not enough places left | Fewer places remain than people you selected |
| A time slot has just filled up | Someone booked the last place; choose another |
| Your household is already registered | Open your existing registration instead |
| There is no ticket for someone | Their age is not covered — contact the organisers |
