# Your profile and household

**My profile** (in the menu, or your account menu) is your own record.

## What you can change

Each section has an **Edit** button:

- **Contact** — phone and WhatsApp numbers
- **Work and education** — industry, job title, education, LinkedIn and
  Facebook links
- **Family history**

## What you cannot change yourself

Your name, date of birth, gotra, caste, thikana, membership tier and chapter
are kept by the Membership Secretary. Use [Contact us](/guide/contact.md) to
ask for a correction.

## Your household

Under **Household** you can add your spouse and your children, and keep their
details up to date.

Your household matters for two things:

- **Events** — you register everyone in your household together, in one go.
  Only people listed here can be added to your registration.
- **Certificates** — a child's volunteer certificate appears on your profile.

Children do not have their own sign-in.

## Certificates

Certificates uploaded for you or your children are listed at the bottom of the
page. See [Certificates](/guide/certificates.md).
