# Joining

Membership is open to the community, and every application is reviewed by an
officer. Becoming a member takes three steps, which you can complete in any
order after applying.

## 1. Apply

Choose **Join** at the top of the portal and fill in the form: your name and
date of birth, your gotra, caste and thikana, your contact details and address,
and the names of two members who can vouch for you. You can add your spouse and
children now or later.

Your address decides your chapter — you do not pick one.

## 2. Confirm your email and choose a password

Straight after you apply, we email you a link. Open it to confirm the address
is yours and to choose your password. The link is valid for 24 hours; if it
has expired, sign-in will offer to send a new one.

Until you confirm, your application is not shown to anyone for review.

## 3. Wait for approval

The Membership Secretary reviews your application. You will get an email when
it is approved — or if they need more information, or cannot approve it.

## 4. Pay your dues

Sign in and open **My membership**. If dues apply to your membership tier,
choose the **Pay … now** button; you are taken to a secure payment page and
brought back afterwards. You can pay before or after you are approved.

## Following your progress

**My membership** shows the three steps and which are done:

| Step | Done when |
|---|---|
| Email confirmed | You have opened the link we emailed |
| Application approved | An officer has approved it |
| Membership dues settled | Your payment has been received |

When all three are done you are an active member, you receive your Member ID,
and the rest of the portal — the directory, events, your files — opens up.

While you wait you can already sign in, see this page, and complete
[your profile](/guide/profile.md).
