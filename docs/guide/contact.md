# Contacting us

Choose **Contact us** at the top of the portal, write your question or concern,
and send it. It goes by email to the organisation's portal address, and
someone will reply to you by email.

Use it to:

- ask for a correction to your name, date of birth, lineage details, tier or
  chapter;
- ask to be removed from an event after you have paid;
- ask about a certificate, or about anything in the portal that is not
  working for you.

You can use the form whether or not you are signed in.
