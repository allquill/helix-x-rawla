# Volunteering

## Offering to help

You volunteer when you [register for an event](/guide/registering.md): tick
**will volunteer at this event** for yourself, your children, or both.

You do not choose a task or a time. The organisers plan the activities and
contact volunteers directly before the event.

## Your hours

After the event, the organisers enter the hours each volunteer served. Once
the event is closed, your hours appear on your registration and count towards
Top Volunteers.

## Top volunteers

**Volunteers** in the menu lists the community's top volunteers, with thanks.

- Rank by **hours served** or by **number of events**.
- Filter by **chapter**, and show **youth** or **adults** only.

If you have left the member directory, your household's volunteers keep their
place and their hours but are shown as "Private volunteer". See
[Privacy](/guide/privacy.md).

## Certificates

Volunteers — young people especially — can be given a certificate for school
or college applications. See [Certificates](/guide/certificates.md).
