# Your files

The portal gives each member a private place to keep files.

## My files

**My files** in the menu. Here you can:

- **Upload** files — drag them in or choose them
- organise them in **folders**
- **rename**, **move**, **replace** and **delete** them
- open a file to **preview** or **download** it

Files are private: nobody else can see them unless you share them.

## Sharing

Open a file or folder and choose **Share** to give access to particular
people, or to everyone holding a role. You choose whether they can only view,
or also edit. You can change or remove a share at any time.

## Shared with me

**Shared with me** lists what others have shared with you.

## Limits

Files can be up to 50 MB.

## Not seeing "My files"?

It is available to active members. If you have just been given access, sign
out and sign back in.
